I am **jimichen**. I work on algorithms and systems architecture.

After years in other languages, I started using Rust in projects. The hardest step was shifting out of familiar programming habits and forming a Rust way of thinking.

Since then, I've been looking for a single thread to make sense of that shift, hoping to reduce the friction of the transition and make the learning curve less steep. Rust Root is my attempt to write it down.

## Contact and feedback

- **Issues and discussion**: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/jimichenx/rustroot/issues)
