## About the author

I am **jimichen**. For more than 20 years, I have worked across algorithm research, system architecture, and engineering leadership. Over the past decade, I have been a founder—running a profitable small company and now working independently while building new ventures. Through every change in role, my love for the craft of programming has stayed constant, and I still write core code by hand every day.

Two years ago, I picked up Rust and began building new projects with it. Even with two decades of engineering intuition, crossing Rust's learning curve was humbling. Along the way, I kept detailed notes on every place where the language fought my existing mental models. Notes like these have a short shelf life: once a mechanism clicks, the curse of knowledge sets in and you forget why it ever felt hard. While those friction points were still fresh, I turned them into this open guide—deriving Rust from first principles rather than memorizing rules.
